# Code of Conduct

JevChess is for everyone who enjoys chess, code, or both. We want this to be a welcoming, harassment-free community regardless of experience level, age, background, identity or ability.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give constructive feedback about code, not about people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Credit others' work and ideas.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language
- Personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information
- Sustained disruption of discussions

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and PRs that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors who repeatedly do so.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately (for example by email, as listed on the repository's profile) instead of raising it in a public issue. Reports will be handled promptly and confidentially.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
